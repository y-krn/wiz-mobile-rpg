## Decision rules

- Do not select `PRODUCTION-GRADE` unless the relevant exact-head/device and
  critical journey evidence is actually observed and major blockers are
  dispositioned.
- Do not select `TOP-TIER EVIDENCED` without relevant independent L3 and
  matched comparative L4 evidence.
- Do not select `WORLD-BEST CLAIM DEFENSIBLE WITH SCOPE` without appropriate
  L4 and L5 evidence, a named competitor set, a named platform/date/scope, and
  explicit contradictory and missing evidence.
- Any unobserved required gate keeps the affected claim at `NOT READY` or
  limits its scope; it never becomes PASS by aggregation.
- A five-person qualitative pilot, a single aggregate score, completion time,
  or NPS cannot establish any of the final statuses on its own.
